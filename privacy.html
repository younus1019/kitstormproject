01
INTRODUCTION

Your Privacy Comes First.

KitStorm respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide when using our website, requesting a quotation, placing a custom teamwear order, or contacting us.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, how it may be protected, and the choices available to you when interacting with KitStorm.

02
INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Information You Choose to Share.

Depending on how you interact with KitStorm, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide through forms, enquiries, quotation requests, and other communications.

Contact Information

Your name, email address, phone number, company or team information, and other details you provide when contacting us.

Order Information

Details relating to your custom teamwear requirements, product selections, quantities, designs, sizes, and specifications.

Enquiry Information

Information contained in messages, quotation requests, project discussions, or other communications with KitStorm.

Website Information

Basic technical or usage information may be collected when you interact with our website.

03
HOW WE USE INFORMATION

Information Used to Serve Your Team.

Information provided to KitStorm may be used to understand your requirements and provide the services you request.

  • Respond to enquiries and quotation requests.
  • Understand custom teamwear and apparel requirements.
  • Prepare and communicate quotation information.
  • Process and manage custom orders.
  • Communicate about projects, orders, and services.
  • Improve our website, services, and customer experience.
04
CUSTOM ORDERS

Built Around Your Requirements.

Custom teamwear orders may require specific information about your team, organisation, apparel requirements, designs, quantities, sizes, colours, branding, and other production specifications.

This information is used to understand your project, communicate with you, prepare quotations, and support the fulfilment of your requested order.

Custom project information

Please only provide information that is necessary for your teamwear enquiry or order.

05

Your Information Is Handled Responsibly.

KitStorm does not intend to sell personal information for unrelated commercial purposes.

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to provide requested services, operate the website, fulfil an order, communicate with you, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Service Support Information may be used by relevant service providers where necessary to support requested services.
Legal Requirements Information may be disclosed where required by law or valid legal process.

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand website usage, remember preferences, and improve the overall user experience.

KitStorm takes reasonable measures to protect information provided through our website and business communications.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal information you have provided to KitStorm.
